05 — Signature Project

Lipi
Coffee

Ahmedabad · 2026

"The owner asked for something that moves."

Faced with a large, empty wall at the heart of Lipi Coffee's interior, we listened not to the brief, but to the space itself. What emerged was a sweeping mural of rhythmic waves — organic forms that rise and recede like breath, rendered in earthy pigments that echo the warmth of hand-poured coffee.

The wall no longer exists. In its place, a living landscape that draws every guest's gaze and holds it.
